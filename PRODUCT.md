# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students learning introductory physics through visual, hands-on experiments on desktop and mobile devices.

## Product Purpose

Physics PlayLab turns core physics concepts into interactive lessons, deterministic simulations, quizzes, and progress-based island exploration. Success means learners can see formulas reflected in motion instead of relying on memorization alone.

## Brand Personality

Playful, clear, and technically credible. The experience should feel like a focused learning lab with game-like energy, not a marketing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-generated decoration, gratuitous UI, unstable novelty interactions, dense textbook presentation, and visual changes that distract from the experiment.

## Design Principles

- Show physics happening in real time.
- Keep the active learning task more prominent than decoration.
- Prefer stable, familiar interactions over novelty.
- Preserve consistent workflows across Learn, Lab, Quiz, and Progress.
- Keep responsive and fallback interactions usable without a camera.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support reduced motion, keyboard-visible focus where applicable, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and mouse or touch fallback when hand tracking or camera access is unavailable.
